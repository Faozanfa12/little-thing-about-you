How beautiful you are.

Not just in the obvious way. Not just the kind of beauty people see at first glance. But the gentle kind — the quiet, thoughtful kind that lives in the way you speak, the way you listen, the way you make people feel seen.

Your smile has this soft effect on me. It changes my mood without asking.

You bring a kind of calm into a room, even when the day feels noisy.

There is something gentle about the way you carry yourself and the way you treat people.

You are one of those people who can be so simple and still feel unforgettable.
