If I could give you one thing…

A little joy, a little ease, and a reason to feel warm in your chest.

A space where you can relax, be real, and never have to perform for anyone.

Someone who notices the quiet things, the effort, and the light you carry.

Someone who keeps choosing you with intention, not just on a good day.

And maybe… I could try to be that person.
